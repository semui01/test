⚛ Instructions

Left-click empty space — spawn a free atom / group.

Left-click an atom — attach a new atom / group with the selected bond order.

Right-click an atom — delete it and its bonds (long-press on touch).

Shift-click two atoms — bond existing atoms (close rings, change bond order). Or use 🔗 Link.

Drag to rotate, scroll / pinch to zoom.

Keys: C H O N F P S L=Cl B=Br I elements · 123 bond order · V view

Panels can be dragged by their headers; click a header to collapse it.
